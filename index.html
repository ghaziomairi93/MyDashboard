📚 User Guide

📍 Location Shared by all widgets

  • One location drives everything — weather, clock, greeting, prayer times, calendar timezone, and background theme
  • Tap the Set Location button in the greeting or the Change Location button on any widget
  • Search any city, town, or country worldwide
  • Location saved locally and persists across visits

🌦 Weather + ⏱ Clock

  • Weather from Open-Meteo using your saved location
  • Clock synced to your location’s timezone, milliseconds shown
  • Background theme changes with time-of-day and weather condition

💰 Crypto Live

  • BTC, ETH, SOL, HNT via CoinGecko free API
  • 24h price change shown — green up, red down
  • Auto-refreshes every 5 minutes. Tap ↻ to refresh now

💲 Forex & Metals Live

  • USD/EUR rate, Gold and Silver per troy oz
  • Auto-refreshes every 5 minutes. Tap ↻ to refresh now

☀️ Prayer Times

  • Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha via Aladhan API
  • Next upcoming prayer is highlighted in teal
  • Requires location to be set
  • Tap Change Location to update

📰 Headlines

  • Top 5 news headlines from NYT RSS
  • Tap any headline to open the full article
  • Tap ↻ to refresh

📅 Mini Calendar

  • Tap any day to sync Reminders and Google Calendar to that date
  • Use ‹ › to navigate months
  • Today highlighted in purple, selected day in cyan

🔔 Reminders

  • Date/time picker sets due date and time
  • Tap + to add a task, checkbox marks done, 🗑 deletes it
  • Clear Done removes all completed tasks at once

📅 Google Calendar

  • Sign In connects your Google account via OAuth
  • Date picker + 7-day bar to navigate
  • + Add creates events in your Google Calendar
  • Tap an event to open Google Calendar app (iOS) or web
  • 🗑 deletes event from server immediately

✉ Outlook Inbox

  • Sign In redirects to Microsoft login
  • Shows 15 most recent unread emails
  • Tap email to open in native mail app on iOS
  • Session persists across page refreshes

🗑 Clear My Data

  • Clears all tasks, location, countdown, sessions, and cached data
  • Cannot be undone
