📚 User Guide

🌦 Weather Top Left

  • Tap to search any city, town or country worldwide
  • Real-time temperature and condition via Open-Meteo
  • Background theme changes with time-of-day & weather
  • Night: stars + moon  |  Day: sun + sky  |  Rain: drops  |  Snow: flakes  |  Storm: lightning
  • Location saved locally and persists across visits

⏳ Live Clock

  • Synced to your chosen city’s timezone
  • Milliseconds shown for precision

📊 Stats

  • Tap Unread Mail → jumps to Outlook inbox
  • Tap Events Today → jumps to Google Calendar

⏳ Countdown New

  • Tap + Add Event — enter name, date, optional time
  • Under 24h: live HH:MM:SS  |  Over 24h: Days + Hours
  • Tap ✎ Change Event to edit
  • Persists locally across visits

💡 Wisdom Quote

  • Tap or ↻ for a new random quote
  • Saved locally and persists across visits

🔔 Reminders

  • Date/time picker sets due date
  • + opens Add Task modal, checkbox marks done, 🗑 deletes
  • Clear Done removes all completed tasks

📅 Google Calendar

  • Sign In connects your Google account
  • Date picker + 7-day bar to navigate days
  • + Add creates events directly in Google Calendar
  • Tap event → opens Google Calendar app on iOS
  • 🗑 deletes event from server immediately

✉ Outlook Inbox

  • Sign In → Microsoft login → shows 15 unread emails
  • Tap email → opens native mail app on iOS
  • Session persists across page refreshes

🗑 Clear My Data

  • Tap → confirm → clears everything including sessions
